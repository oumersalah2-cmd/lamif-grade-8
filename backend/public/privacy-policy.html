1. Afaan Oromoo

Ragaalee Nuti Funaannu

  • Odeeffannoo Galmee: Maqaa, lakkoofsa bilbilaa, godina mana barumsaa fi maqaa mana barumsaa.
  • Nagahee Kafaltii: Suuraa nagahee Telebirr ykn CBE kaffaltii qormaataa mirkaneessuuf.
  • Qabxii fi Sochii Qormaataa: Qabxiiwwan qormaata shaakalaa offline irratti hojjatte.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Yoo fedhii keetiin eyyemte qofa dogoggora teeknikaa sirreessuuf funaanama.

Yeroo Ragaan Turu (Data Retention)

Suuraaleen nagahee kafaltii guyyaa 45 booda ofumaan serverii irraa ni haqamu.

Mirga Ragaa Haquu

Herrega kee yoo haquu barbaadde, appii keessaa (Settings) ykn websaayitii /delete-account irratti lakkoofsa bilbilaa galchuun yeroo kamiyyuu haquu dandeessa.

2. English Summary

Information We Collect

Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep collects student names, phone numbers, school details, payment receipts for registration verification, and offline exam scores.

Retention & Deletion

Payment receipts are automatically purged after 45 days. Account deletion can be performed at any time in-app or via our web portal at https://api.lamif.org/delete-account.
