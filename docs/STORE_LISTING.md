# Google Play Store Listing Specification

This document contains the complete bilingual metadata, graphic asset specifications, and release notes for **Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep** version 1.0.0.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To comply with Google Play Developer Policies:
> - Character counts are strictly within limits (Title: ≤ 30 chars, Short Description: ≤ 80 chars, Full Description: ≤ 4000 chars).
> - Superlatives and unauthorized claims such as *"Official"*, *"Government"*, *"#1"*, or *"Guaranteed Pass"* are strictly omitted.

### Full Description (Up to 4000 characters)
```text
Appilikeeshiniin Lamif Kutaa 8 barattoota kutaa 8ffaa Oromiyaa qormaata naannoo fi seensa mana barumsaa bultii addaa Ifa Boruutiif qopheessuuf kan tolfamedha.

1. Shaakala Barnoota Hundaa:
- Herrega (Mathematics)
- Afaan Oromoo
- Saayinsii Waliigalaa (General Science)
- Saayinsii Hawaasaa (Social Studies)
- Barnoota Lammummaa (Citizenship)
- Afaan Ingilizii (English Language)
- Ogummaa fi Teeknoolojii (Career & ICT)

2. Qormaata Yeroon Daangeffame (Mock Exam):
- Qormaata akka naannoo qormaata dhugaatti sa'aatii lakkaa'uun dandeettii kee madaali.
- Qabxii kee akkuma xumirteen ibsa deebii sirrii wajjin battalatti argadhu.

3. Tarree Gaaffilee fi Mallattoo (Question Palette):
- Gaaffii shakkite yeroo barbaaddetti mallatteessi (Flag for review).
- Tarree gaaffilee (Question Palette) fayyadamuun gaaffii feete salphatti filadhu.

4. Shallaggii Qabxii fi Karoora (Grade Calculator & Target Planner):
- Qabxii kee galchuun giddu-galeessa (average) kee herregadhu.
- Karoora qabxii qopheeffachuun barnoota hafan keessatti qabxii meeqa akka si barbaachisu beeki.
- Qabxii qormaata shaakalaa irraa ofumaan guuttachuuf qophiidha.

5. Xiinxala Mata-duree Dadhabaa (Weak-Topic Analysis):
- Qabxii kee kanaan duraa irraa mata-dureewwan shaakala dabalataa barbaadan adda baasa.
- Mata-duree dadhabaa irratti qormaata addaa hojjedhu.

6. Guutummaatti Offline Hojjeta:
- Gaaffileen hundi bilbila kee keessatti waan kuufamaniif intarneetiin yoo hin jirrellee ni hojjeta.

Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep waliin qophii kee har'uma jalqabi!
```

## 2. Store Listing Copy (English Version)

### App Title (Max 30 characters - 26 characters)
`Lamif Grade 8: Exam Prep`

### Short Description (Max 80 characters - 76 characters)
`Practice Grade 8 regional exams offline with answers, timer and score calculator.`

### Full Description (Up to 4000 characters)
```text
Lamif Grade 8 is a dedicated, offline-first exam preparation app designed for Grade 8 students in Oromia preparing for regional ministry exams and Ifa Boru boarding school entrance tests.

Practice anywhere, anytime completely offline without data costs!

KEY FEATURES:

1. Comprehensive Subject Coverage:
- Mathematics (Herrega)
- Afaan Oromoo
- General Science (Saayinsii Waliigalaa)
- Social Studies (Saayinsii Hawaasaa)
- Citizenship (Barnoota Lammummaa)
- English Language
- Career & Technical Education (ICT)

2. Timed Mock Exams:
- Practice under simulated exam conditions with an interactive countdown timer.
- Instant automated grading with clear explanations for each question.

3. Question Palette & Review Tools:
- Flag challenging questions for later review during practice sessions.
- Question palette grid enables instant navigation across all questions.

4. Grade Calculator & Target Planner:
- Calculate total marks, average percentages, and performance achievement tiers.
- Set a target average score and determine exactly what marks you need on remaining subjects.
- Auto-fill your best scores directly from completed practice exams.

5. Weak-Topic Analysis:
- Automatically detects subjects and topics where accuracy falls below 65%.
- Launch targeted practice sessions specifically for topics needing improvement.

6. Offline-First Architecture:
- All questions, attempts, and study progress are stored locally on your device.
- Zero data required during study sessions; automatic background synchronization when online.

Prepare with confidence for Grade 8 success with Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep!
```
