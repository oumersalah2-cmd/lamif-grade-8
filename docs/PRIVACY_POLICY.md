## 1. Afaan Oromoo (Oromo Version)

### A. Ragaalee Nuti Funaannu
Appilikeeshiniin **Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep** ragaalee armaan gadii qofa tajaajila qormaataa fi barnootaa siif kennuuf funaana:
1. **Odeeffannoo Eenyummaa fi Galmee**: Maqaa kee, lakkoofsa bilbilaa, godina mana barumsaa fi maqaa mana barumsaa keetii.
2. **Nagahee Kafaltii**: Suuraa iskiriinshatii (screenshot) nagahee Telebirr ykn Baankii Daldala Itiyoophiyaa (CBE) kan kaffaltii qormaataa mirkaneessuuf ergitu.
3. **Qabxii fi Sochii Qormaataa**: Qabxii qormaata shaakalaa, yeroo qormaata irratti dabarsite, fi sadarkaa adeemsa barnoota keetii.
4. **Ragaalee Teeknikaa fi Dogoggoraa (Crash Logs)**: Yoo fedhii keetiin eyyemte qofa, dogoggora appii sirreessuuf ragaan teeknikaa (model bilbilaa fi Android version) anonymized ta'ee ergama. Maqaan ykn lakkoofsi bilbilaa gabaasa kana keessatti hin hammatamu.

### B. Akkaataa Ragaan Itti Fayyadamu
- Qabxii fi adeemsa qayyabannoo kee offline irratti kuusee yeroo intarneetiin dhufu PostgreSQL database wajjin wal-simsiisuuf.
- Eenyummaa fi galmee kaffaltii kee mirkaneessuuf.

### C. Yeroo Ragaan Turu (Data Retention)
- **Suuraaleen Nagahee Kafaltii**: Guyyaa 45 booda ofumaan serverii irraa guutummaatti ni haqamu.
- **Qabxiileen Qormaataa**: Hamma barataan herrega isaa haquutti ni tura.

### D. Mirga Ragaa Haquu (Data Deletion Rights)
Barataan kamiyyuu yeroo barbaadetti herrega isaa fi ragaa isaa hunda haquuf mirga qaba:
- **Appilikeeshinii keessatti**: Qindaa'ina Dhuunfaa (Settings -> Privacy) keessatti cuqaasuun "Herrega Koo Haqi" filachuun PIN dijiitii 6 mirkaneessuun dhaabbataan haquu dandeessa.
- **Websaayitii irratti**: Fuula [https://api.lamif.org/delete-account](https://api.lamif.org/delete-account) irratti lakkoofsa bilbilaa fi PIN seensaa keessan mirkaneessuun yeroo kamiyyuu ragaa kee guutummaatti haquu dandeessa. PIN barbaachisuun nama biraan akka herregni kee hin haqamne eega.

## 2. English Version

### A. Information We Collect
**Lamif Kutaa 8 Prep** collects only the data strictly necessary to deliver offline exam preparation and track progress:
1. **Registration & Contact Data**: Student name, mobile phone number, school administrative zone, and school name.
2. **Security PIN**: 6-digit numeric PIN stored strictly as a salted non-reversible cryptographic hash (never stored or transmitted in plain text).
3. **Payment Verification Data**: Mobile screenshot of the Telebirr or Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) bank transfer receipt.
4. **Academic & Performance Data**: Exam scores, timestamps, test duration, and subject mastery levels synced from the local Isar database.
5. **Diagnostic & Crash Telemetry**: Strictly non-personal technical logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics only when explicitly consented to in settings. No personal identifiers are linked.

### B. How We Use Information
- To authenticate students offline using their salted PIN hash.
- To record practice exam attempts and calculate academic progress.
- To verify student registration fee payments.
- To detect software crashes and ensure stability on budget Android devices.

### C. Data Retention & Automatic Deletion Policy
- **Payment Receipt Images**: Automatically and permanently deleted from our server after **45 days** via automated cron cleanup.
- **Student Profile & Exam Records**: Retained until the user requests account deletion.

### D. User Rights & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)
Users have the absolute right to delete their account and associated data at any time:
- **In-App**: Navigate to `Settings -> Privacy Settings -> Delete Account` and verify your 6-digit PIN.
- **Web-Based Deletion Portal**: Submit your registered phone number and 6-digit PIN at `https://api.lamif.org/delete-account`. Requiring PIN verification protects against malicious account deletion by unauthorized parties. All user records, exam attempts, and receipts will be purged immediately.

### E. Child & Teen Safety
Our content is tailored for Grade 8 students (ages 13–17). We do not display third-party advertisements, do not collect location data, and do not sell student data under any circumstances.
